Where this list comes from

The core items come from the UK government's Prepare campaign, which asks households to keep a torch, radio, power bank, spare batteries, first aid kit, hand sanitiser and wipes, bottled water, food that needs no cooking, and enough medication for several days.

Water amounts follow the same guidance: 2.5 to 3 litres per person per day just to drink, or 10 litres per person per day to cover cooking and washing too.

Three things that cost nothing

  1. Sign up for flood warnings and check your long-term flood risk.
  2. Join your energy and water supplier's Priority Services Register if someone at home is vulnerable.
  3. Download the government's household emergency plan, fill it in and keep it with your kit.
